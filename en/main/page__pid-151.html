DATA CENTER LIQUID COOLING

From cooling water piping to the server rack,
liquid cooling under single-point responsibility

AI KOREA designs, supplies, builds and commissions the entire liquid cooling loop: primary cooling water piping, CDU, manifold and rack-level cold plates. It is built on the piping technology we developed handling secondary battery electrolyte with precision.

Liquid Cooling Loop and Our Scope

One company covers the loop from heat rejection to the chip, so responsibility is never split between vendors.

What Sets AI KOREA Apart

We are not an equipment reseller. We design and build the piping ourselves.

01BIM-based piping design

We model the whole loop in 3D to catch clashes before construction. The as-built model is used for maintenance during operation.

02Precision control from electrolyte handling

We apply to cooling water loops the temperature and flow control experience gained keeping electrolyte with a 33℃ flash point below 10℃.

03Leak-free piping

Precision welding of STS 304/316, prefab modules and zone-by-zone leak detection reduce the risk of piping that runs above servers.

04Projects in four countries

We bring large-plant piping experience from the United States, Hungary, Poland and China to data centers.
