Benefits

Everything you need
to stay focused on your work

AI KOREA supports a fulfilling life for every member of our team through flexible, practical benefits.

Work Support

We cover meals, work expenses, business travel and quarterly team dinners.

  • Lunch, dinner and snacks provided
  • Reimbursement of work-related expenses
  • Travel expenses for domestic and overseas business trips
  • Quarterly team dinner allowance

Education & Health

We support regular check-ups, comprehensive health screenings, health counseling and professional certifications.

  • Regular check-ups and tiered comprehensive health screenings
  • Health counseling
  • Support for professional certifications

Recognition & Rewards

We recognize long service, outstanding employees, project results and company-wide performance.

  • Long-service awards and leave
  • Outstanding employee awards
  • Project performance awards
  • Incentives based on company-wide performance (at least once a year)

Life & Community

We mark family occasions and holidays, run an employee council and a dormitory, and support in-house clubs.

  • Allowances and leave for family occasions
  • Holiday gifts
  • Employee council for labor-management welfare
  • Support for in-house clubs
  • Company dormitory
